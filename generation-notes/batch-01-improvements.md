# Batch 01 — Packaging Improvements

Where the lesson plan diverged from 1-section-per-lesson packaging, with reasoning.

Teacher sections covered: §§2–20 (19 sections). Lessons produced: 22.

## Section splits

### §4 → L3 + L4

§4 contains two unrelated things: (a) the `-tion → -cion` transfer pattern and (b) the first grammar construction (`quiero` + infinitive + reflexive `me`). Splitting lets L3 stay a pure transfer-pattern lesson (~5 confirmation exercises) and L4 focus on the first grammar rules without competing for attention.

### §5 → L5 + L6

Similar reason. §5 has (a) `yo` emphasis + DO pronouns postverbal + rolling rr — one of the densest grammar sections — and (b) the `-ence/-ance → -encia/-ancia` transfer pattern at the tail. L5 is the grammar lesson; L6 is a short pure-transfer lesson.

### §14 → L15 + L16

§14 combines (a) `se` (irregular saber yo), (b) `salgo` (another yo-go verb), (c) `tengo que` + infinitive, and (d) `pose → poner` transfer with the `poner` family. The first three are grammar-tight (modal + irregulars); the last is a pure transfer pattern. L15 = grammar; L16 = transfer + `poner` lemma.

## Section merges

Intentionally none. Each teacher section gets ≥1 lesson.

## New lessons (gap-fills)

No lesson was inserted purely to fill a gap in batch 1. Gap-fills (negation at L1, articles at L9) were added as rules inside lessons the teacher had already carved, rather than standalone lessons. See `batch-01-gaps.md`.

## Lesson-order divergence from teacher-section order

None. Every teacher section's content appears in a lesson with the same or adjacent ordinal, in original sequence.

Within a single teacher section, content is sometimes regrouped (e.g., §12's `-ity → -idad` transfer pattern is grouped with the same lesson as its -ion-feminine and vender content, rather than split out), but this is within-section packaging and doesn't affect the grammar-concept introduction order.

## Transfer-pattern placement

All 9 transfer patterns are introduced in lessons 1–16 (first third / halfway through the batch), per the spec's interleaving directive:

| Pattern | Lesson | Teacher § |
|---|---|---|
| suffix-al-stays | 1 | §2 |
| suffix-ant-ent-to-e | 2 | §3 |
| suffix-ible-able-stays | 2 | §3 |
| suffix-mente-equals-ly | 2 | §3 |
| suffix-tion-to-cion | 3 | §4 |
| suffix-ence-ance-to-encia-ancia | 6 | §5 |
| suffix-ity-to-idad | 13 | §12 |
| suffix-ary-to-ario | 14 | §13 |
| stem-pose-to-poner | 16 | §14 |

Lessons 2 and 13 each bundle multiple transfer patterns. Not split — the patterns are quick (5 exercises apiece) and related. Keeping them together makes for a thicker but thematically coherent transfer-pattern lesson.

See `batch-01-sequencing-concerns.md` for notes on why `-ity` and `-ary` weren't front-loaded further.
