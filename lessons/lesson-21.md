`ir` is the most important motion verb in Spanish, and it's irregular across the board. Once you can ask "where are you going?", you can start having real conversations.

## `a donde` = to where

English asks "where are you going?". Spanish is closer to "to where are you going?".

- a donde vas
- a donde van

## `ir` is fully irregular (learn it as chunks)

You already know `voy`. Add the key pieces you hear all the time:

- vas = you go
- va = he/she goes
- vamos = we go
- van = they go

And it plugs straight into your modal patterns:

- quiero ir
- tengo que ir

## `esta noche` = tonight

Literally "this night":

- Tengo que ir esta noche

## -e nouns: you often cannot guess gender from the ending

With many `-e` nouns, gender is not predictable. You learn it by **seeing the article** or just memorizing the phrase.

- la noche
- la carne
