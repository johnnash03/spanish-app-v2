Three more cognate patterns and one pronunciation note. No new grammar — each rule here is "recognize the suffix, convert it, done."

## -ant / -ent → add an `-e`

- important → importante
- different → diferente
- constant → constante

## -ible / -able stay as-is

- possible → posible *(note: one `s`)*
- durable → durable

## -ly → -mente

English `-ly` adverbs become Spanish `-mente`. It stacks onto the adjective — often one you already got for free from a previous rule.

- really → realmente
- constantly → constantemente
- illegally → ilegalmente

## Pronunciation: `j` is kh

The Spanish `j` is not an English j. It's a scratchy back-of-throat sound — the *kh* in Scottish *loch* or German *Bach*. Spelled `j`, said *kh*.
