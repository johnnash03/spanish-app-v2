Another high-yield noun pattern. Just like `-tion → -cion`, this one turns big English nouns into instant Spanish nouns.

## -ence / -ance → -encia / -ancia

Swap the ending:

- difference → diferencia
- influence → influencia
- existence → existencia
- importance → importancia

More examples the same way:

- consistence → consistencia
- incongruence → incongruencia

One nice stacking trick: you can often get the adjective first, then the noun.

- important → importante → importancia

Once you have the pattern, any English `-ence` / `-ance` noun is basically already Spanish.
