Now you can say "we". For regular `-ar` verbs, the we-form is one of the simplest patterns in the language.

## We-form for `-ar`: add `-mos` (you hear `-amos`)

Take the "I" form ending (`-o`) and replace it with the we ending (`-amos`):

- tomo → tomamos
- hablo → hablamos

So:

- tomamos — we take / we drink

And you can immediately use it with the new nouns:

- No tomamos alcohol — We don't drink alcohol

## Stem-changers do NOT split in "we"

Here's the important twist: when you say "we", the stress moves to the ending, so the stem doesn't need to do its split.

That's why you get:

- pensamos *(not `pienamos`)*
- encontramos *(not `encuentramos`)*
- mostramos *(not `muestramos`)*
- cerramos *(not `cierramos`)*

It feels like a cheat code: stem changes everywhere... except in we.

One more simple contrast to keep in your ear:

- yo: encuentro / pienso / muestro / cierro
- nosotros: encontramos / pensamos / mostramos / cerramos

The ending takes the stress, so the stem stays normal.
