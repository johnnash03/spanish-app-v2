Two upgrades: (1) you can say "I" when you want emphasis, and (2) you can say "it / him / her / you" without repeating a noun.

## `yo` is for emphasis

Spanish can drop "I" because the verb already tells you who is acting. Add `yo` when you want to point at yourself.

- Yo no quiero cancelar — *I* don't want to cancel
- No quiero cancelar — I don't want to cancel

## Object pronouns: `lo`, `la`, `me`, `te`

- `lo` = it / him / you (formal, masculine)
- `la` = it / her / you (formal, feminine)
- `me` = me
- `te` = you (informal)

## The pronoun sticks to the to-verb

When the object comes after a to-verb, attach it to the end. No space.

- Quiero cancelarlo — I want to cancel it
- No quiero obligarte — I don't want to force you
- Quiero informarme — I want to inform myself

## Pronunciation note: `rr` is rolled

When you see `rr` inside a word, roll it.
