This is a transfer-pattern lesson with one super-useful verb: `poner` (to put).

## `poner` is a "yo-go" verb

- Pongo — I put
- Lo pongo — I put it / I'm putting it

## English `-pose` verbs become Spanish `-poner`

If you see `-pose` in English, Spanish often gives you the `-poner` family:

- compose → componer
- suppose → suponer
- impose → imponer
- expose → exponer
- oppose → oponer

And you can already use it in real speech:

- Supongo que si — I suppose so / I guess so
