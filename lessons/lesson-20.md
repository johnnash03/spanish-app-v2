You already know the we-form for `-ar` (`-amos`). Now you get the only place where `-er` and `-ir` stop pretending to be the same: the we-form.

## We-form for `-er`: `-emos`

- perder → perdemos
- comer → comemos
- vender → vendemos

## We-form for `-ir`: `-imos`

- vivir → vivimos
- venir → venimos

## Stem-changers still do NOT split in we

This keeps paying off. Even a stem-changer like `preferir` stays calm in we:

- prefiero / prefiere / prefieren ... but **preferimos**

So when you hear a form like `preferimos`, don't go hunting for a stem change. In we, the ending carries the stress.

## `siempre` and word order

Spanish is relaxed about where you place `siempre` (always):

- Siempre perdemos
- Perdemos siempre

## `aqui` / `aca` = here

Two common ways to say "here": `aqui` and `aca`.

You can drop them into normal sentences right away:

- Vivimos aqui
- Vivimos aca
