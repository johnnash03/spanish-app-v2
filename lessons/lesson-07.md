Two quick upgrades: a new helper verb (`intentar`) and a pronunciation rule that saves you effort.

## `intentar` = to try

Use it the same way as `quiero`: directly with a to-verb.

- Intento publicar — I am trying to publish
- Intento continuar — I am trying to continue
- Intento imitar — I am trying to imitate
- Intento administrar — I am trying to administer

And if you need `lo` (it), it still sticks to the to-verb:

- Intento publicarlo — I am trying to publish it
- Intento continuarlo — I am trying to continue it
- No intento publicarlo — I am not trying to publish it

You can use the same idea with other to-verbs you already have:

- Intento visitarlo — I am trying to visit him
- Quiero visitarlo — I want to visit him

## The letter `h` is silent

Spanish `h` is completely silent. You write it, but you don't pronounce it.

- horrible — say it like "orrible"
