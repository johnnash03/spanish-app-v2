You now have "do/make", "can", and a clean way to ask "what ...?" without any word-order gymnastics.

## `hacer` = to do / to make

- Lo hace — He is doing it

## `que` = what

Spanish can ask "what are you doing?" with no extra helper words:

- Que haces — What are you doing / How are you

## `puedo` / `puedes` = I can / you can

`poder` takes a to-verb directly.

- Puedes venir — Can you come
- Puedo venir — I can come

## Informal you (`tu`) adds `-s`

Take the he/she/it form and add `-s`:

- hace → haces
- puede → puedes

## `esperar` and object pronouns

- Me esperan — They are waiting for me

And your new motion verbs plug right into what you already know:

- llegar → llego
- salir → salgo

## -ary → -ario

- necessary → necesario
- contrary → contrario
