Three useful upgrades: `ver` has an irregular "I" form, you can express "going to" cleanly, and `nos` can mean "each other".

## `ver`: the I-form is `veo`

`ver` doesn't follow the regular pattern in the "I" form.

- veo = I see
- ve = he/she sees
- ven = they see
- vemos = we see

## Future with `ir a` = going to

This is the most natural "going to" future:

- Vamos a ver... — We are going to see...

And the pronoun can either attach to the to-verb or move in front of the first verb:

- Vas a vernos pronto
- Nos vas a ver pronto

## `pronto` and `la semana`

- pronto = soon
- la semana = the week

`la semana que viene` is a common way to say "next week".

## `nos vemos` = we see each other

Here `nos` is not "us" as an object. It means "each other":

- Nos vemos la semana que viene — We see each other next week
